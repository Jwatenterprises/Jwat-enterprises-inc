How the match works

Lenders mostly look at the same few things: how long you've been in business, your monthly revenue, your credit, and how fast you need the money. If you're not there yet, the smartest first move is usually building business credit or opening a business account, not applying and getting turned down. The quiz follows that order. It's a starting point, not a loan decision: the partner makes the final call.
