What Makes Intro Kids Different

Most money books for kids feel like homework. Intro Kids books feel like games. The reader makes choices, sees consequences, earns XP, unlocks ranks, and tracks their money like a player tracking their score.

The lessons are real — budgeting, saving, earning, spending, credit, investing basics — but they come through story, not lecture.

For parents, that means less fighting about reading and more actual learning. For educators and homeschoolers, that means a ready-to-use resource with built-in discussion points.
