Parent Questions

Intro Kids! FAQ

What are the Intro Kids! books?

Intro Kids! is a three-book financial literacy series for kids and teens ages 10–18, written by Tiesha M. Walters and Wayne Walters. The books are The Money Game, The Lemonade Empire and Money Before 18. Each one teaches real money skills like earning, saving, budgeting and credit through interactive, choose-your-path stories instead of worksheets.

What ages are the Intro Kids! books for?

The Money Game is for ages 10–15. The Lemonade Empire is for ages 10–15. Money Before 18 is for teens ages 13–18.

How much do the Intro Kids! books cost?

Each book is $5.99 as an instant-download digital PDF on Gumroad, or $10.99 as a printed paperback on Lulu. All three digital books together cost $17.97.

Where can I buy the Intro Kids! books?

Digital PDFs are sold on Gumroad at jwatbiz.gumroad.com for instant download. Printed paperbacks are sold on the Lulu Bookstore. The books are sold directly through these two stores.

Which Intro Kids! book should my child start with?

For ages 10–12, start with The Money Game for everyday money decisions, or The Lemonade Empire if your child is interested in starting a business. For teens 13 and up, start with Money Before 18, which covers income, credit and investing.

How can I teach my kids about money?

Start with everyday decisions your child already makes: give them a small grocery budget, talk through what a free app really costs, or break down a first paycheck together. Short, real-life conversations work better than lectures. The free Intro Kids! parent guide, 7 Money Conversations to Start This Week, gives age-specific scripts for each of these.
